Quad

For IITM BS students, by an IITM BS student

The campus our online degree doesn't have.

Over 30,000 of us study for the IIT Madras BS degree from cities and towns all over India. We almost never meet. Quad is a community app only for verified students: you sign up with your @ds.study.iitm.ac.in email. Then you can join subject groups, OPPE and end-term prep circles, hackathon teams, fest crews and city meetups. Each one has posts, a real-time group chat, file sharing and direct messages. When a group gets busy, ✨ Catch me up uses AI to tell you what you missed in seconds.

Judges and visitors: “Try the live demo” logs you into a shared demo account with sample communities. Quad is an unofficial student project and is not affiliated with IIT Madras.

Why not just WhatsApp?

📣 Important stuff gets buried

A course group gets hundreds of messages before a quiz or OPPE. The one message with the deadline or the notes link scrolls away and nobody can find it again.

🕵️ Anyone with the link can join

Group links get forwarded everywhere, so spammers and "paid help" sellers get in. On Quad, every account is checked against a student email address.

🧭 No campus, so no way to find your people

In an online degree, nobody bumps into a study partner in the corridor. Quad lets you browse every community, and find students taking your course, at your level, in your city.

What you can do on Quad

✅ Verified students only

You sign up with your student email and confirm it with a one-time code. Your batch is read from your roll number, so nobody can fake it.

🏘️ Communities for everything

Courses, exam prep, hackathons, fests, clubs and city meetups. Anyone can start one, and anyone can find and join it.

📝 Posts that stay visible

Announcements, questions and notes go in a feed with likes and comments, so they don't get lost in chat.

💬 Real-time group chat

Every community has a live chat. You can share PDFs, images and files up to 15 MB.

✉️ Direct messages

Message any verified student, like a teammate, a senior or a classmate, without swapping phone numbers.

✨ Catch me up

Missed 200 messages? In one click, AI on Amazon Bedrock lists the highlights, deadlines, unanswered questions and files you need.

Built on AWS, serverless from end to end

  • Amazon CloudFront + S3 serve this site from edge locations close to students.
  • Amazon Cognito handles sign-up with email verification. A Lambda trigger blocks any email that isn't a student address.
  • Amazon API Gateway runs an HTTP API for the app and a WebSocket API for live chat.
  • AWS Lambda (Python) runs all the app logic, with no servers to manage.
  • Amazon DynamoDB stores profiles, communities, posts and messages in a single table.
  • Amazon S3 stores shared files. Browsers upload directly using short-lived presigned links.
  • Amazon Bedrock powers ✨ Catch me up.
  • AWS CDK defines all of this as code. It was built with Claude Code, a coding agent connected to the AWS account.
